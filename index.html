Useful CSV tools without complicated software.

CSV files are one of the simplest ways to store and exchange tabular data. They are commonly used for spreadsheets, exports, databases, analytics and moving information between different applications. CSVTools gives you focused browser-based utilities for everyday CSV tasks.

Whether you need to inspect a file, remove duplicate rows, merge datasets, split a large file or convert CSV to another format, the goal is simple: get the job done without installing desktop software or creating an account.

Common questions.

What is a CSV file?

A CSV file stores tabular data as plain text, with values separated by commas or another delimiter.

How do I open a CSV file?

Choose a CSV file above to preview its rows and columns directly in your browser.

Can I convert CSV to JSON?

Yes. CSV to JSON is one of the conversion tools planned for the CSVTools toolbox.

Are uploaded CSV files stored?

The browser-based tools are designed to process supported files locally on your device.
